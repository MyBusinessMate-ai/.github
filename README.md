## Featured Project — AI Business Audit Automation

An AI-powered business audit workflow designed using n8n
to analyse a business's digital presence and identify
growth opportunities.

The workflow covers:

- Website and SEO analysis
- Google Business Profile analysis
- Social media performance assessment
- Competitor research
- Digital presence gap identification
- AI-generated business audit reports
- Personalised growth recommendations
- Lead qualification and sales automation

Our objective is to transform manual business audits
into scalable, intelligent, and automated workflows.
